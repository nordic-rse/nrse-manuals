Nordic-RSE association annual meeting

Link to statutes: https://nordic-rse.org/about/governance/

Practicalities (per the statutes)

  • Held annually, January–March.

  • The board must invite members by e-mail no later than seven days before the meeting.

  • The statutes allow members to attend in person, or remotely by mail, online, or other technical tools — in practice, our meeting is held remotely.

  • The meeting is held in English.

  • Each member has one vote; decisions are the proposals that receive more than half the vote, unless the statutes say otherwise (statute amendments and dissolution require a three-quarters (3/4) majority vote).

The times are approximate. The meeting is held January–March.

Meeting parts

For the full agenda, invitation text, and meeting notes, use annual-meeting-template.md — it follows the statutory agenda (Section 10) point by point.

Board nominations

The new board is elected at the annual meeting. Any member who has paid the membership fee for the current year can stand. See onboarding.md for the board’s composition and roles.

Members can put themselves forward in two ways:

  • before the meeting, by e-mail to secretary@nordic-rse.org, or

  • at the annual meeting itself, when the chair calls for volunteers.

Before the meeting, the board reaches out to possible new board members and prepares a suggested composition, which is pre-filled in the meeting notes. The meeting can change it.

The call for candidates goes out with the invitation to the annual meeting (see the invitation template).

Diversity considerations when preparing the formation of the new board:

  • research field

  • job title

  • career stage

  • gender

  • country coverage
