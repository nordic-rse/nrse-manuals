Meetings

Check our website for current times and links for meetings: https://nordic-rse.org/join/

Coffee break

Informal weekly events where everyone can join to have a cup of your favorite beverage, share experiences, discuss the news or also just vent about the daily frustrations of live as a Research Software Engineering enthusiast. This is very informal, no agenda or decisions are made here. Everyone is welcome; bring your friends!

Community meeting

Held monthly (see nordic-rse.org/join for date and time). This is where we drive Nordic-RSE forward. Coordination of events and initiatives, reporting on past initiatives and discussion of ideas and opportunities for future collaborations. Everyone welcome, this is a possibilty to shape what Nordic-RSE is and does!

This is our guideline for every meeting:

  • Before meeting

    • Chair reminds people about meeting and to add to agenda in Zulip

  • Intro for new people :sunflower:

  • Action points followup

  • Choose a chair for the next meeting

    • chair tasks: send reminder about meeting in zulip, prepare agenda (+ remind others to add topics), keep the meeting on topic and under 60 minutes

  • List of participants

  • Mark actions, to be done by whom and until when

  • Update news on the nordic-rse.org, if suitable

  • Note on discussions made

Topic meetings

Besides the regular community meetings, we sometimes hold separate meetings focused on a single topic or initiative, for example planning an upcoming Nordic-RSE conference. These are organized by the people working on that topic, as often as the work needs. If you’d like to help with an initiative, ask in the community meeting.

Board meeting

The board meets when it considers it necessary, or when at least half of the board members request it. In practice this is usually about twice a year. Only board members attend, and the chair or secretary sends the invitation. See board/onboarding.md for quorum and how the board makes decisions.

Annual meeting

This is the event for the whole community. It includes reports on status and finances of the association and the election of a new board. Every member of the association (those who paid the membership fee for the current year) have a voting right. Everyone is welcome to attend.
