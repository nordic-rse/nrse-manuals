# Nordic-RSE contacts

The Nordic-RSE is an open community always in flux. 
See also the [community page](community.md) for who we are, what Nordic-RSE works on, and how to get involved.

You can meet a lot of the active community members in our [Zulip chat](https://coderefinery.zulipchat.com).

The main contact point for everyone is our shared e-mail address: secretary@nordic-rse.org. 

All board members have access to that e-mail address.
