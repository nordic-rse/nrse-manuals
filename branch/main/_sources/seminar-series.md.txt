# Seminar series

:::{note}
The seminar series is currently on break. It ran from August 2021 to May 2023 and is waiting for someone to pick it up again. If you're interested, bring it up in the community meeting or on Zulip.
:::

The public page, including the full list of past seminars and recordings, is at [nordic-rse.org/events/seminar-series](https://nordic-rse.org/events/seminar-series/).

## What it is

The Nordic-RSE Research Software Seminar Series is a series of online talks, demos and discussions on research software topics. It is open to everyone interested in RSE topics, whether or not they are an RSE or based in the Nordics. Speakers have come from universities and companies, both from within the community and invited from outside.

- **Frequency**: the aim was one seminar a month. In practice it ran 11 sessions between August 2021 and May 2023.
- **Format**: typically a talk followed by discussion, e.g. 40 minutes talk + 20 minutes discussion, or 15 + 15. Sessions can be anything from 10 to 180 minutes.
- **Platform**: Zoom, with one HackMD document per session for connection details and asynchronous questions and discussion (e.g. [seminar-May-2023](https://hackmd.io/@nordic-rse/seminar-May-2023)).
- **Recordings**: most talks were recorded and published on the [Nordic-RSE YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC8OyVrmJEuT2lrH7zXoBrhQ).

## Proposing a seminar

Anyone can propose a topic or a speaker, including themselves, by opening an issue in the website repository with the [seminar series event template](https://github.com/nordic-rse/nordic-rse.github.io/issues/new?template=seminar-series-event.md). The template asks for:

- title
- whether you will run it yourself or are suggesting someone else
- abstract / short description
- event type: talk, workshop / code-along, or other
- duration
- suggested date(s)
- questions for the organizers

## Running a seminar


- [ ] Pick a proposal from the issues, or invite a speaker, and agree on a date and format.
- [ ] Offer the speaker support: past speakers were offered mentoring for preparing their talk.
- [ ] Add the seminar to the [seminar series page](https://nordic-rse.org/events/seminar-series/) on the website.
- [ ] Add it to the `series` calendar in the [calendar repository](https://github.com/nordic-rse/calendar) (see [website/index.md](website/index.md#calendar)).
- [ ] Create the session's HackMD document in the [Nordic-RSE HackMD team](https://hackmd.io/team/nordic-rse), with the Zoom details.
- [ ] Announce it on Zulip, the mailing list and social media (see [tools-accounts.md](tools-accounts.md)).
- [ ] Ask the speaker for permission to record, and record the session.
- [ ] Afterwards, publish the recording on YouTube and link it from the seminar series page.


## Picking it up again

The main thing the series needs is one or two people to coordinate it: collect and invite speakers, schedule sessions and do the announcements. The infrastructure (Zoom, HackMD, the website page, the calendar and the YouTube channel) is all still in place.
