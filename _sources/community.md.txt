# The Nordic-RSE community

## Nordic-RSE Community statement

(This is a first draft)

The Nordic Research Software Engineering community is primarily made up of Research Software Engineers (RSE) and RSE enthusiasts based in the Nordic and Baltic countries: Iceland, Norway, Denmark, Sweden, Finland, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania — as well as anyone else interested in the topic, at any career stage.

As an association, Nordic-RSE works on promoting the careers of RSE by:

* being a networking platform for specialized research supporters,
* provide a community and professional development network for RSEs
  (**in particular supporting career development**),
* organizing events to bring RSEs and related people together, and
* supporting those who want to bring RSE services and skills to their
  community (in other words, provide a RSE service).

The ways to interact with the community (chat, coffee breaks, meetings, membership) are covered in [onboarding.md](onboarding.md).

We are a young association and there is plenty of room to get involved with your own ideas - please get in touch.

Many of us are active in CodeRefinery, which focuses on teaching RSE-like skills to researchers.

We are not directly funded by anything, but CodeRefinery supports us by sharing the chat and other infrastructure. Multiple organizations support us by allowing their staff to contribute to our activities. 

All community spaces (chat, meetings, events) are covered by our [Code of Conduct](https://nordic-rse.org/about/code-of-conduct/).

## Community map

The [Nordic-RSE community map](https://nordic-rse.org/communities/) shows individual RSEs as well as organized RSE groups (at universities, national labs, research institutes, etc.) across the Nordics and Baltics. Anyone who feels a connection to the RSE spirit can be included — a formal RSE job title isn't required.

To add yourself or your group, submit a pull request to [nordic-rse/map](https://github.com/nordic-rse/map), editing [data.yml](https://github.com/nordic-rse/map/blob/main/data.yml). See the repo's [README](https://github.com/nordic-rse/map/blob/main/README.md) for submission guidelines and privacy considerations.

## Local and regional groups

Besides individual RSEs, the map also lists organized local and regional RSE groups (e.g. university or national RSE teams). If you are organized in such a group and would like it added, [get in touch](https://nordic-rse.org/join/) with Nordic-RSE.
