# Topic collection

Nordic-RSE is run by volunteers, and everything we do started with someone who had an idea or wanted to help. This page collects topics that have come up in meetings or the chat as things we could work on together. You're very welcome to join in — no experience, membership or big time commitment needed.

## Working on a topic

If one of the topics below interests you, mention it in the [chat](chat.md) or bring it up in the next [community meeting](meetings.md#community-meeting), where the currently active topics are discussed. You don't have to take on a whole topic by yourself: helping out with a small part is just as valuable, and others are happy to get you started.

## Suggesting a new topic

Have an idea of your own? Share it in the chat or at the monthly community meeting. We're happy to hear about every idea, even if you don't have time to lead it yourself.

Ideas are most likely to happen when they have high impact, are low cost (in time to run and maintain), and have someone to lead them — maybe you!

## Topics

- Buddy program (link senior members with new members to welcome to the community and have a point of contact)
- Identify membership benefits and put on website
- RSE info presentation/flyer for anyone to share at their organization
- Move topics of general interest from chat discussion to some public place, e.g. blog
- Continue [RSE seminar series](seminar-series.md)
- State of Nordic-RSE blog post
- State of RSE funding in Nordic countries: research and blog post (or more?)
- Nordic Research Software Institute
- Set up better member management system
