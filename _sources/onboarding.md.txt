# New team member onboarding 

## Must know and do

The **Nordic Research Software Engineers** association (in short, Nordic-RSE) 
is a grass-root association that aims at providing a professional network to 
**Research Software Engineers** (RSE) in the Nordics and Baltics. While the term "RSE" is 
not as of yet of common use here, Nordic-RSE is currently a community for 
people who operate at the interface between research and digital technologies 
(software, computing, data). We operate on an inclusivity base: if you think 
that the above description is you, then you are welcome in our association!

See [community.md](community.md) for the association's aims, and our [Code of Conduct](https://nordic-rse.org/about/code-of-conduct/), which applies to all our spaces.

### Connect

- **Chat - Zulip**: [https://coderefinery.zulipchat.com](https://coderefinery.zulipchat.com) — our main communication platform, shared with the CodeRefinery community. This makes you part of the community.
    - introduce yourself in #greetings>introductions
    - lots of optional channels, check them out!
- **Weekly coffee break**: Thursdays at 9:00 Europe/Stockholm (10:00 Europe/Helsinki), same [Zoom link](https://aalto.zoom.us/j/62426845282). Not a planning meeting — an informal chat and a great way to get to know who is around.
- **Bi-weekly meetings**: [notes and agenda](https://hackmd.io/@nordic-rse/biweekly), [Zoom link](https://aalto.zoom.us/j/62426845282), every second Thursday at 13:00 Europe/Stockholm (starting 2025-08-21). These are our planning meetings — join if you would like to help with activities or shape the association's future. Everybody welcome; you do not have to be a member of the association to join.
- **Mailing list**: [sign up here](https://forms.gle/paPBnvsw5GAXUzBN6) — best way to stay in contact without interacting much. No need to be a member of the association to sign up.
- **Association membership**: [membership form](https://forms.gle/bgvYgFHrVMFrccHt6) and [membership fee](https://nordic-rse.org/about/membership-fee) — see [membership.md](membership.md) for how to join and what it enables.

### Accounts

If you're joining the organizing team, please get yourself an account on the following platforms: 

- Zulip
- GitHub
- HackMD

On GitHub we have an organization that you will be added to to have access to few private repositories and be able to merge pull requests etc. On HackMD we have a team that you will be added to to have write access to some team notes.
