# Communications

What Nordic-RSE puts out, how often, and for whom. Each item links to the details.

## Overview

| What | Frequency | Channel | For whom | Purpose | Details |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newsletter | Infrequent | E-mail to the mailing list | Mailing list subscribers | Information sharing | [Newsletter](#newsletter) below |
| Social media posts | When there's something to share | Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn | Followers and the wider RSE community | Announcing events and blog posts, visibility | [Social media](#social-media) below |
| Blog / news | When posts come in | [nordic-rse.org/blog](https://nordic-rse.org/blog/) | The wider RSE community | News, event reports, RSE topics | [website/blog.md](website/blog.md) |

In addition, we have regular meetings (the weekly coffee break, the monthly community meeting and the annual meeting) and the annual conference, where things may be communicated as well. See [meetings.md](meetings.md) and [conference/index.md](conference/index.md) for what each one is for. Their dates, times and Zoom links live on [nordic-rse.org](https://nordic-rse.org/).

## Channels

For account details and who has access, see [tools-accounts.md](tools-accounts.md).

- **Zulip** (`#nordic-rse` on the CodeRefinery Zulip): day-to-day communication, meeting reminders and agendas, quick announcements.
- **Mailing list**: for people who want to stay informed without being on the chat. Used for the newsletter, annual meeting invitations and membership reminders.
- **E-mail from secretary@nordic-rse.org**: official association communication, e.g. annual meeting invitations, which must go out by e-mail at least seven days before the meeting.
- **Website**: the canonical place for events, meeting times and public information.
- **Social media** (Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn): announcements and sharing blog posts.

## Social media

What typically gets posted:

- New blog posts, once published on the website
- Event announcements: conferences, seminars, calls for contributions
- Conference posts, following the list in [conference/tools-templates.md](conference/tools-templates.md#social-media-posts)

Graphics and social media card templates are in [nordic-rse-materials](https://github.com/nordic-rse/nordic-rse-materials).

Some members of the board have access and do the posting. 

## Announcing something new

A rough checklist for anything that should reach the whole community:

- [ ] Put it on the website first (event page, blog post, or calendar entry), so the other channels can link to it.
- [ ] Post on Zulip.
- [ ] Post on social media.
- [ ] For bigger items, include it in the next newsletter, or send a separate mailing list e-mail.
