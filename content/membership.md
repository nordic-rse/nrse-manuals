# Nordic-RSE membership and what it enables

For who can join, how to join, the membership fee, and why to become a member, see nordic-rse.org's [Get involved](https://nordic-rse.org/join/) and [membership fee](https://nordic-rse.org/about/membership-fee) pages — those are the public-facing, up-to-date versions to point a prospective member to. This page covers the organizer/board-facing side: the governance mechanics behind membership, and how organizations can support us.

## Organizational support

Organizations can also support Nordic-RSE, for example by:

- sponsoring specific activities or services, locally or Nordic-wide, or
- letting staff contribute to Nordic-RSE on work time.

Reach out if you have other ideas for how your organization could help.

## Rights as a member

- **Voting**: each member has one vote at association meetings — see [board/annual-meeting.md](board/annual-meeting.md) for the full voting/quorum mechanics.
- **Annual/general assembly**: members are invited to participate — see [board/annual-meeting.md](board/annual-meeting.md) for timing and practicalities.
- **Board membership**: members can be elected to the board at the annual meeting — see [board/onboarding.md](board/onboarding.md) for board composition and how it works.

## Losing membership

The board can remove a member who acts against the association's purpose, violates the code of conduct, or fails to pay their fee. Members have the right to know the reason for their removal and to express objections in writing.

## E-mail templates

Note: We do not currently send either of the e-mails suggested below. 

## Welcome e-mail 

Send this after someone fills in the [membership form](https://forms.gle/bgvYgFHrVMFrccHt6) (which itself only shows an on-screen "Thank you for registering in the Nordic RSE group!" message, and collects Name, Title, Email, Country/City of Residence, and Affiliation — no e-mail is sent automatically).

```
Subject: Welcome to Nordic-RSE!

Hi <name>,

Thanks for your interest in becoming a member of the Nordic-RSE association! To become a full member, please pay
the membership fee (10€/year) if you haven't already — see how at
https://nordic-rse.org/about/membership-fee.

Once that's done, you're a full member, which means:
- you get a vote at our annual meeting,
- you can be elected to the board, and
- you're helping fund the community and our activities.

A few other ways to get involved, if you haven't already:
- Join our Zulip chat: https://coderefinery.zulipchat.com
- Sign up to the mailing list: https://forms.gle/paPBnvsw5GAXUzBN6
- Come to our weekly coffee break or biweekly meeting — see
  https://nordic-rse.org/join/ for times.

Welcome aboard!

<sender name>, on behalf of the Nordic-RSE board
```


## Yearly reminder to pay the membership fee 

Send this to members who have not paid the membership fee for the current year yet. Send this in good time before the annul meeting. Only send this to the members who have not paid for the current year!

```
Subject: Renew your Nordic-RSE membership for this year

Hi <name>,

We noticed that you have been a member of the Nordic-RSE association previously, but we have not yet received your membership fee for the current year.

If you would like to continue as a full member, please renew your membership by paying the annual membership fee (€10/year). Information on how to pay can be found here:

https://nordic-rse.org/about/membership-fee

As a full member, you:

- can vote at our annual meeting,
- are eligible to stand for election to the board, and
- directly support the Nordic-RSE community and its activities.

We would be delighted to have you continue as part of the association.

If you have already paid recently, please disregard this message. If you have any questions about your membership status, payment or would like to be removed from the member registry, feel free to get in touch.

No matter how you decide on the membershiop, remember that you can stay involved in the community:

- Join our Zulip chat: https://coderefinery.zulipchat.com
- Sign up for the mailing list: https://forms.gle/paPBnvsw5GAXUzBN6
- Join our weekly coffee breaks or meetings: https://nordic-rse.org/join/

Thank you for being part of the Nordic-RSE community, and we hope to see you again as a member this year.

Best regards,

<sender name>
 On behalf of the Nordic-RSE Board
```
